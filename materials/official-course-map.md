# Official course map - learn-ai-finance-with-phoebe

**Course:** AI + Finance - how finance practitioners embrace AI
**Scope:** Broad finance function INCLUDING markets (FP&A, accounting, controllership, treasury, audit, tax, risk, credit, and markets/investing at survey depth). Deep markets/trading + investment mechanics are deferred to future courses #11 (AI + Investment) and #12 (AI + Trading).
**Arc:** Topic-session · two-track (a1-a6 leader, b1-b10 builder).
**Coverage bar:** each session teaches ~80% of the mapped sources' working content. Certificates, graded assessments, and vendor tooling stay with the official programs - said honestly on each page.
**Build mode:** course-taking loop PAUSED (2026-07-23), so built directly from verified public sources below (no learner-notes step this build).

## Hard rails (bake into every relevant page)

- **"AI assists, the licensed human decides."** No investment advice, no automated credit/trading decisions. Loud on a3, b8, b9.
- **Data privacy rail:** never paste MNPI, client PII, or unpublished financials into public/consumer AI tools. Loud on b1, b10.
- **AI-washing rail:** SEC/FINRA scrutiny on overstating AI capability. Mention a3, b9.

## Not covered by design (say so honestly)

- Portfolio construction math, factor models, personal financial planning advice → future #11.
- Building/fine-tuning proprietary ML models from scratch → learn-intro-ml + learn-classification-regression.
- Vendor-specific certifications, graded exams, and paid tooling (Datarails, Bloomberg GPT, etc.) stay with the official programs.
